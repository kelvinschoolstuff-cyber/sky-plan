# AgriCast usability test plan

Purpose: check that the workflow (location, crop, calendar, forecast, guidance, save) is understandable and useful.
This is a usability and context check. It does not replace agronomic verification of the calendar data.

## Participants
5 to 8 representative users per round: smallholder farmers growing maize or beans if reachable, plus extension officers,
agricultural students or group leaders. Record role, county, age band, device and connectivity. Get verbal or written
consent, explain that the test checks the app and not the person, and keep notes anonymous.

## Tasks (observe silently; do not help unless the person is stuck for 2 minutes)
1. Find the 7-day forecast for a chosen Kenyan location.
2. Find maize planting information for your county.
3. Find bean planting information for your county.
4. Say what the guidance suggests for an upcoming wet or dry period, and whether it is a guarantee.
5. Say whether the calendar entry is for your exact county, and where it came from.
6. Create an account, add a farm and a crop plan, and set a reminder.

## Record per task
Completed without help (yes/no), time taken, errors or wrong turns, quotes, and a 1 to 5 ease rating.

## Follow-up questions
Does the season and crop information match what you know locally? Which words were unclear? What would you check
before acting on this? Was it clear what is a forecast, a calendar and general guidance?

## Accessibility checks
Keyboard-only run through all pages, 200% zoom, colour contrast, screen reader labels on forms, and use on a low-end phone
over a slow connection.

## Success targets (adjust with your supervisor)
At least 80% of participants complete tasks 1 to 3 unaided; every participant can tell a forecast from a calendar entry;
no one reads the guidance as a guarantee.

## Report
Summarise findings, severity, changes made, and limitations (sample size, who was recruited, no expert validation).
